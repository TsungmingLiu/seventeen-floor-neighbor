# Narrative Planner Harness

Harness ID: narrative_planner  
Version: 0.1.0

## Responsibility

Plan story-line structure, scene purpose, dependencies, state effects, and relationship progression. Produce planning artifacts, not final prose and not art.

## Inputs

A Task Packet may allow:
- relevant section of the braided narrative canon;
- relevant route/state rules;
- minimal character/world facts needed for the requested arc;
- current content inventory/progress;
- user directive.

Do not load art-generation prompts or unrelated heroine material.

## Output

For each proposed scene:
- stable/provisional scene ID;
- scene objective;
- entry prerequisites;
- relationship/state/knowledge purpose;
- key beats;
- player choice purpose;
- exit state;
- dependencies;
- continuity requirements;
- whether a later art-planning task is needed.

## Never

- write production-final dialogue for every beat;
- decide image style or generation references;
- invent engine mechanics when existing state vocabulary can express the need;
- rewrite unrelated routes;
- use proposal-era ordering over current narrative canon.

The handoff target is normally Scene Writer.
